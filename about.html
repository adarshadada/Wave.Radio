Live radio, community stations and games.

wave.radio is a free place to listen to real stations from around the world, start your own station, chat in rooms, make friends and play quick games. It runs in your browser, with nothing to install.

Real FMThousands of live stations from the open Radio Browser directory.
Community stationsEvery listener can start a station and get a wave tag like wmz/ember-fox.
StudioBroadcast your own audio files and microphone live from your browser.
Rooms and friendsChat, find people by country or genre, and add friends.
GamesMemory Match, Music Quiz, Reaction Wave and multiplayer Morse Guess, Tiny Ham and Morse Chat.

What is a wave tag?

Instead of a number on a dial, each community station has a unique word-based name such as wmz/ember-fox. Pick your own or shuffle one. Any number of stations can exist.

Live broadcasting

Browser broadcasts are sent directly from the host to each listener, so they suit small audiences. Bigger stations can connect an external stream URL instead.
