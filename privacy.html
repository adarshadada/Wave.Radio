Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This page explains what wave.radio collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our provider, Supabase) and the username you choose.
  • Profile: optional display name, bio, link, country, accent colour, profile picture and banner.
  • Content you create: stations, rooms, chat messages, friend requests, upvotes and uploaded images.
  • On your device: game scores and settings are saved in your browser (local storage).

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your profile and station to other people, deliver chat and live audio, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your data.

Microphone and audio

The microphone is only used when a station owner switches it on in Studio. Audio is sent live to listeners and is not recorded or stored by wave.radio.

Other services

  • Supabase stores accounts and community data and powers live chat and signalling.
  • Radio Browser provides station listings. Radio streams come directly from each broadcaster, who may see your IP address.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries.
  • STUN/TURN relay servers help connect live audio between devices.
  • Advertising: if ads are turned on, Google AdSense may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can manage this in your Google ad settings.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time from the profile menu.
  • Delete your account from your profile. This removes your profile, station, rooms, messages, friends and votes. Uploaded images may take longer to be fully removed from storage.
  • Clear your browser data to remove saved scores.

Children

wave.radio is not meant for children under 13. Please do not create an account if you are under 13.

Safety

Admins can remove content, suspend accounts and end live broadcasts that break the rules. Be kind and do not share anything illegal or harmful.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions? Sign in to the app and use Send feedback in your profile.
